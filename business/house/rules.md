# House rules - what is true for every client

One line per rule, dated, in the words it was said in. Read before
writing anything for anyone.

A correction belongs here only when it is about how we work, not about
one business. "Do not promise a reply rate" is a house rule. "Do not
mention our Warsaw office" is that client's. When the same correction
arrives from a second client, move it up here and say so in one line.
